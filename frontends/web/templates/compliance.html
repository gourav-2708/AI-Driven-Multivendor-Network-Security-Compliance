The percentage is the share of controls that pass an audit or are attested in the control register, out of all assessed controls. A control no rule checks becomes Attested when an administrator records it as implemented, adds evidence, and keeps its review date current. Controls recorded as not applicable are left out. Open a control to see or change its owner, evidence and review date.
